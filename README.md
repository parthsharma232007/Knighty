## ✨ What Knighty Does

Knighty lives directly on your laptop screen as an interactive overlay companion that reacts to your everyday workflow:

| State | Trigger | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🚶 **Walking** | Normal idle roaming | Strolls casually across your desktop and taskbar. |
| 🏃 **Running** | Active exploration | Sprints across the screen when full of energy. |
| 🦘 **Excited Jump** | App opened or closed | Jumps in reaction whenever you launch or close an application. |
| 💤 **Corner Rest** | No recent interaction | Walks down to the bottom corner of your screen and sits quietly until you interact again. |
